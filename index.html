Privacy Policy

PredictKick · Last updated: 26 August 2026

In short: PredictKick does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. You can use the app without creating an account. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Who we are

PredictKick ("the app") is developed and operated by Davide Lombardozzi ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy or about your data, contact us at davlombcloud@gmail.com.

What PredictKick is

PredictKick is an analytical tool that publishes football match predictions generated from statistical models and publicly available bookmaker odds. It is not a bookmaker and not a gambling service: no real money can be wagered, deposited or won inside the app. The app is intended for users aged 18 or over.

What data we collect

DataWhyWhere it is stored
An anonymous account identifier (a random ID generated by Firebase Authentication) To remember whether your device has an active VIP subscription, without asking you to register Google Firebase
Your VIP subscription status and its expiry date To unlock VIP content on your device Google Firebase
A notification token To send you a daily notice when new predictions are published, if you allow notifications Google Firebase
App preferences and local statistics (for example: that you accepted the terms, your prediction streak) To keep the app working the way you left it Only on your device
We never receive your payment details. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. Card numbers and billing information are processed by Google and are never sent to, or stored by, PredictKick.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files or any other content on your device
  • Any advertising identifier — the app contains no advertising

Anonymous accounts

When you first open the app, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous identifier for your device. This identifier is a random string: it is not linked to your name, your email address or your Google account, and it cannot be used to identify you personally. Its only purpose is to remember your subscription status.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use the following service providers:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Messaging) — stores the anonymous identifier and subscription status described above. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscription payments. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — the app downloads its typefaces from Google's font service the first time it runs.

Match data, team logos and bookmaker odds shown in the app are retrieved from a third-party sports data provider. These requests are made by our servers, not by your device, so no information about you is involved.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app subscribes your device to a general announcement channel and we send one message a day when new predictions are published. The same message is sent to every subscriber: we do not use notifications to target individual users. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

How long we keep data

The anonymous identifier and subscription status are kept for as long as the app is installed and the subscription record is relevant. Data stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or to object to its processing. Because the data we hold is anonymous, we may need you to provide the identifier shown in the app in order to locate your record.

To delete your data: uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device. To also have the subscription record deleted from our systems, write to us at the contact address above and we will remove it.

Children

PredictKick is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a minor has used the app, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Where the change is significant, we will also announce it inside the app.
